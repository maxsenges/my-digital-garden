# Mission 4 — How should research co-evolve with AI?

If transformative AI has fully enabled the Next Economy, research becomes both the biggest lever and the largest risk surface. As discussed, the economics agenda will have extended beyond growth; we will have a research agenda with decisive themes like wealth distribution, power concentration, information integrity, safety and the transition dynamics that can trigger social crises.

## Dual financing architecture

For large-scale, long-horizon problems — climate adaptation, public health preparedness, resilient infrastructure, education system redesign — that require patient capital and coordination, mission-driven public-private partnership investments make most sense. This is not state versus market; it is a public-private portfolio (Mazzucato, 2013).

Open science and shared research infrastructure becomes the default choice when AI systems become a core research substrate, as access to compute, data and evaluation tooling determines who can produce insights. A public and interoperable AI research infrastructure — offering compute credits, shared benchmarks, open repositories and replicability requirements — is essential for research on questions of political and systemic interest. The principle Romer (1990) established holds: knowledge is non-rivalrous; the more it is shared, the more value it creates.

## How researchers work with AI

AI accelerates literature mapping, hypothesis generation, simulation and parts of experimentation. But this increases rather than decreases the premium on epistemic discipline: reproducibility, ensuring dataset integrity and governance of model behavior. Research in the Next Economy is less like solitary genius work and more like human–AI co-production inside well-governed collaborative pipelines, consistent with the peer-production logic of Plurality.

The human researcher's irreplaceable role is teleological and contextual: *What is the right question? Which values guide the search? What are the implications of a finding for actual people and communities?* That is the organ of natality that AI cannot substitute.
