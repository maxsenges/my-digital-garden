## Abstract

The Next Economy is not shaped by technological determinism. It is a humanistic societal operating system story about the unfolding of human potential toward a desirable future. The recent and extrapolated advances in AI are expanding the feasible set of human action faster than our private, organizational and societal institutions can absorb. The risk is the Oblomov effect: the slow surrender of our human agency through convenience. This essay argues for a deliberate redesign of our societal operating system (SOS), proposed through three interlocking layers. Part One establishes the theoretical foundation: augmentation over automation, humans as agents not resources and a realistic-utopian north star for the transition. Part Two develops four Mazzucato-style societal missions that structure the path from today's economy to the Next Economy. Part Three offers an enterprise transformation playbook; namely concrete tools for organizations ready to lead rather than lag the shift.

**Keywords:** Next Economy, Societal Operating System, Augmentation, Oblomov Effect, Unfolding Human Potential, Capability Approach, Mission Economy, Transformation Stack, Oikonomia, Plurality, Realistic Utopianism, Entrepreneurial Mindset
