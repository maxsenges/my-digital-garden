# What happens if we actually do this? — Turning Freedom into Creation

Oblomov's tragedy is not a lack of intelligence. It is the slow erosion of agency in the conditions of abundance — or at least abundance of time-and-attention-consuming offers. That erosion is not cured by better tools. It is cured by better education, better institutions and a cultural ecosystem that treats entrepreneurship (genuine initiative) as the normal mode of contribution.

![Berlin Friedrichstraße Utopia 2048](https://demokratischer-salon.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Berlin_Friedrichstrasse-e1728920577142.jpg)
*Berlin Friedrichstraße Utopia 2048 — Aeeroscape & Lino Zeddies, Wikimedia Commons*

AI can expand freedom. The Next Economy, as a societal operating system, should be designed to make freedom productive rather than sedating: a world where AI reduces the "must work" so we can increase the intrinsically motivated; where *Bildung* is revived as *the* civic aspiration; where entrepreneurship is understood not as a personality type but as a capacity to generate meaningful impact; where democratic participation is a source of everyone's ability to co-shape and be heard, enabled by tools that make it genuinely effective; and where scientifically backed methods (deliberative democracy, NPOV-grounded briefing materials, structured evidence-based discourse) rather than algorithmic propaganda form the basis for collective decision-making.

The models already exist for all dimensions of the Next Economy: from 42 to Amsterdam's Doughnut experiment, from Wikipedia to the Horizon Europe missions. What is missing is the critical mass of people who recognize these as coherent parts of the Next Societal Operating System and who also bring the entrepreneurial conviction that they can help build it.

That is the alchemy of the desirable future we invite you to co-create: designing the 2050 north-star narratives and the business models that convert technological power into the scarce product that markets alone will not produce — lives that are meaningfully lived, in community with others, in stewardship of the world we share.

> ***The Next Economy does not arrive. It is built one enterprise, one community, one deliberate beginning at a time.***
