# What design principles hold the Next Economy together?

The shift to the Next Economy is best understood as a portfolio shift: away from optimizing for profit extraction, toward optimizing for human flourishing. Four design principles hold the system together.

**Education as primary transformation engine.** Not a sector among others, but the decentralized agency-training ground that defines system capabilities like peer-production competence and entrepreneurial agency. Investment in human capability is the highest-return investment a society can make.

**Decentralized but interoperable.** Local autonomy generates resilience; open standards enable collaboration and scalability without monopolized choke points. The internet itself is proof of concept. Plurality extends this principle from technology to governance and culture (Weyl & Tang, 2023).

**Commons + shared assets + private expression.** Some goods function best as commons or public-private partnerships (education, health, core digital infrastructure). Some assets can be shared to reduce waste (tools, vehicles). Private property remains essential where it expresses identity, culture and individual creativity.

**Governance that is machine-readable but human-legitimate.** Rules should be machine-readable and executed consistently, while decision accountability remains human. Auditability, legibility, contestability and participation are non-negotiable conditions for democratic legitimacy in an AI-enabled governance system.

These principles provide the ground for four grand challenges in the spirit of mission-economy thinking (Mazzucato, 2021).
