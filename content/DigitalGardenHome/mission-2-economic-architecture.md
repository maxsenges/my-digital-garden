# Mission 2 — How do we redesign economic architecture beyond GDP?

GDP is a useful accounting tool but an increasingly weak strategic compass in a world where many digital goods approach zero marginal cost, where global capital markets operate at unfathomable magnitudes and speeds and where the main risks are systemic. The Next Economy needs an evolved operational framework — one that is both philosophically grounded and practically compelling to business leaders.

*Oikonomia*, what the Greeks considered the art of good stewardship of the household, is not nostalgia. It is a framing for our missing control system. Aristotle's distinction between *Oikonomia* and *Chrematistics* (wealth accumulation as end in itself) identifies precisely the categorical error of contemporary economics. Three converging frameworks make this operational:

- The **Doughnut model** (Raworth, 2017) defines the safe and just space between nurturing the social foundation and not breaching the ecological ceiling.
- The **Common Good Economy** (Felber, 2010) translates this into corporate governance: capital is a tool, not the goal.
- **Commons peer-production and governance** (Ostrom, 1990; Benkler, 2006) demonstrates that shared resources can be created and managed sustainably across centuries without either privatization or state control.

Two movements show how this philosophy is already being adopted in business practice without sacrificing commercial ambition. **Conscious Capitalism** (Mackey & Sisodia, 2013) argues that business is inherently good and that its highest expression rests on four tenets: a higher purpose beyond profit, stakeholder integration, conscious leadership and a conscious culture. Companies like Patagonia, Costco, Southwest Airlines and the Container Store, operating on these principles, have consistently outperformed their sectors financially while building the trust and loyalty that competitive moats based on opacity cannot replicate. **Beautiful Business** (Morris, 2022; Leberecht, 2015) frames every enterprise as a "fundamental creative journey" whose power rests on integrity, curiosity, elegance, love as connective tissue and magnetic differentiation. These are not soft values; they are precisely the capabilities that AI cannot commoditize.

## Where will value shift in the AI economy?

Organizing markets around human life areas — rather than legacy industries — clarifies where the Next Economy's wealth creation will concentrate. Building on Jacobides, Cennamo & Gawer's (2018) theory of ecosystems as a distinct governance form held together by modularity and non-generic complementarities, the Ecosystemizer framework (Kawohl & Krechting, 2022) maps ten life areas, and Table 1 below assesses their relative trajectories in an AI-saturated economy:

| Ecosystem Domain | Relevance/Jobs in the Next Economy | Business-Model Shift |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Health & Wellbeing | Up (aging + prevention + AI triage) | Treatment transactions → prevention platforms + human care delivery |
| Unfolding Potential (Education) | Up sharply | New credentials → capability products: mastery evidence, apprenticeships, communities |
| Personal Security (digital + societal) | Up sharply | Point solutions → continuous assurance, identity, auditability |
| Entertainment | Up sharply | Content (AI/video/games) ↔ live shows and real-world experiences |
| Personal Mobility | Mixed: efficiency up, labor down | Ownership → shared fleets, orchestration, routing |
| Industry | Output up, labor down | Linear production → flexible, localized, circular systems |
| Living | Mixed | Architecture, real estate, furniture, home improvement |
| Consumption | Mixed | Food, fashion, personal tech — quality > quantity shift |
| Relationships & Community | Up | Matching & facilitation → membership, belonging, verified safety |
| Belief / Mindfulness / Meaning | Up | Content → practice communities, rituals, guided development |
| Sustainability (cross-cutting) | Up (hard constraint) | Green tech and ESG reporting → measurable circularity and zero-emission/regenerative operations |

*Table 1 — Next Economy Scenario Forecast*

The pattern across all domains: value migrates from output volume (cheapened by AI) toward trust, care, creative orchestration, imagination and experience. All of these aspects require irreducibly human capabilities — the qualities that both Conscious Capitalism and Beautiful Business have long argued are also the sources of durable competitive advantage.

For incumbent companies, the transition has a four-step logic:

1. Identify the strengths and weaknesses of your current business model given the Next Economy outlook. (Where do you profit from lock-in, opacity, or externalized costs?)
2. Define your 2050 North Star and adapt or re-invent your business grounded in stewardship for the Next Economy.
3. Move from products to ecosystems. Orchestrate interoperability and partner capacity rather than hoarding it.
4. Energize and train your stakeholders to embrace the transformation journey.
