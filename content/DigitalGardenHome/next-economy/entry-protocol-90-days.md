# Where do I start in the first 90 days? (The Entry Protocol)

**AI Potential Maturity Analysis.** A structured diagnostic mapping the organization's current AI readiness across the five stack layers. It identifies where augmentation is already happening informally, where automation is being mistaken for augmentation and where human-in-the-loop protocols are missing. *Output: a prioritized opportunity map with a 90-day pilot selection.*

**Agency, Strengths & Entrepreneurship Training.** A cohort-based program (typically 3–6 months) grounded in character strengths identification, self-efficacy development and Mindset Alchemy — the deliberate reconstruction of limiting beliefs about AI, change and personal capability. The goal is *agentive literacy*: the ability to decide confidently what to delegate to AI and what to own as human, combined with the entrepreneurial confidence to initiate new solutions rather than waiting for permission.

**Augmentation Pilots.** Three to five concrete workflow redesigns — selected from the Maturity Analysis — in which human–AI collaboration is explicitly designed, measured and iterated. Each pilot includes a human-in-the-loop protocol, a capability-expansion metric (not just efficiency) and a trust checkpoint (does this workflow increase or decrease participant agency?).

These three interventions generate the experiential evidence that makes organizational learning possible. Arguments for the Next Economy do not move incumbents. Demonstrably better results do.
