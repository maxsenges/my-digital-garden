# Which transition archetype fits my organisation?

The stack looks different depending on where an organization starts. Four archetypes cover most incumbent situations:

| Starting Point | Destination | Primary Leverage | First 90-Day Move |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Industrial company (manufacturing, logistics, energy) | AI-enabled adaptive production system | Shift from output volume to outcome quality; circular production models; ecosystem orchestration of supply chain | AI Potential Maturity Analysis of top 10 workflows; identify which tasks are Labor (automate), Work (augment), Action (protect) |
| Service firm (consulting, legal, finance, health) | Outcome-based augmentation provider | Replace billable-hour pricing with outcome contracts; use AI to expand capacity while human judgment commands premium | Pilot one outcome-priced engagement; map where AI augments vs. where human judgment is the product; build client trust scorecard |
| Platform (marketplace, network, media) | Trust infrastructure | Shift from attention extraction to verified relationship facilitation; interoperability commitments; transparency and contestability features | Audit the extraction vector; design one transparency + contestability feature as public commitment |
| Public organization (government, university, NGO) | Mission co-creator and co-leader | Translate political will into co-designed missions; become the Rules-as-Code anchor institution; build participatory infrastructure | Identify one mission where the org can be anchor; begin Rules-as-Code pilot; launch citizen participation design sprint |
